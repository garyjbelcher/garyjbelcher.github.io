I recently finished reading Ironwood by [Michael Connelly](https://www.michaelconnelly.com/). It's the second book in the series featuring Detective Stilwell, and [just as I predicted](https://garyjbelcher.github.io/2025/08/21/nightshade.html), in this one he teams up with Ballard to catch a serial killer. It's another gripping read from one of my all-time favorite authors, and that cliffhanger ending has me on tenterhooks for the next book!
