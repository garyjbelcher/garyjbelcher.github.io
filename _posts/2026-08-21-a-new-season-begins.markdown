It doesn't really feel as though it has ever been away, what with the World Cup finishing just over a month ago, but football is back this weekend with the return of the Premier League. [Villa](https://www.avfc.co.uk/) start their campaign at Brighton on Sunday afternoon. It has been a summer of much change at Villa Park, with multiple important players departing, so this season is sure to be interesting. Last week's [defeat to PSG in the UEFA Super Cup](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cmn4n23z15elt) was disappointing, but the performance was encouraging. I am really looking forward to the new season and of course competing in the Champions League again!
