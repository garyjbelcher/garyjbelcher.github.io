I finished the story in [Marvel's Wolverine](https://www.metacritic.com/game/marvels-wolverine/) on PlayStation 5 last night. I'm not really sure I agree with the reviews calling it average, as I really, really enjoyed it from start to finish. It's a completely different game to Insomniac's previous game, Marvel's Spider-Man 2, but that doesn't mean it's a bad game. I might even play through it again, but first I have that all important Platinum trophy to obtain.
