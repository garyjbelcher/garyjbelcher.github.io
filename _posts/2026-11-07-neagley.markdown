I recently finished watching [Neagley](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33539520/) on Prime Video. It's a spin-off of Reacher that focuses on his army friend Frances Neagley, who works as a private investigator. Overall, I thought the show was excellent, and I really enjoyed how the story unfolded, even if it was a little implausible and predictable at times. The supporting cast was excellent, too. I can't wait for the second season!
