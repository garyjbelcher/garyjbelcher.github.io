Well, it has been a couple of weeks since Bungie - incredibly - announced that, no, Destiny 2 is not ending, and that they are [not done with the franchise](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/nextchapterofbungie). I was beyond thrilled when I read that announcement, as the game has been such a huge part of my life for over a decade. It would be amazing if they announced Festival of the Lost or The Dawning was returning, as they are my two favourite seasonal events, but that seems unlikely.
